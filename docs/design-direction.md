# Website design direction

Reference: Web Design (3).docx, supplied on 6 October 2026. Reviewed all seven mockups: signed-out landing page, registration, login, signed-in landing page, catalogue, cart and tracking.

Apply the lilac/pink gradient header and hero, deep-purple footer, white cards, rounded controls, cake photographs and elegant typography. Admin/Staff screens share the palette with a practical sidebar/table layout; no admin mockup was supplied.

Use K Sweet Kreations Co. Approved collection-only, manual quotations, accounts and status/payment rules override conflicting mockup features. Products, prices and stock photos are preview content. Do not fabricate actual contact information, reviews or promotions.
